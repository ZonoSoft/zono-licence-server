Guides

Step-by-step help to install and use the apps. Managers use Shifenza; team members use Shifenza Team.

Shifenza for WindowsFor managers on a Windows PC or laptop: install, set up your business, plan rotas, clocking, timesheets and payroll. Shifenza for AndroidFor managers on an Android phone or tablet: install, set up your business, plan rotas, clocking, timesheets and payroll. Shifenza Team for WindowsFor team members on a Windows PC or laptop: connect to your work, clock in and out and see your shifts. Shifenza Team for AndroidFor team members on an Android phone: connect to your work, clock in and out and see your shifts.

Employers: see using Shifenza with your staff for what to tell your team.
