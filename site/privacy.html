Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026

This explains what personal information ZonoSoft handles, why, how long it's kept and your rights.

Who we are

ZonoSoft is the trading name of James Turner, a sole trader, of Flat 2, 4 Wooperton Street, Weymouth, Dorset DT4 7DX, United Kingdom. We are the controller of the information described on this page. Contact: ZonoSoft@outlook.com.

Your business's data in the apps

Rotas, staff details, clock times (including where someone was when they clocked in or out), holidays, timesheets and payroll hours are stored on your own devices, encrypted, and, if you choose, in your own OneDrive, Google Drive or network folder. ZonoSoft does not receive, store or have access to this data. Your business is its controller and is responsible for telling its staff how it's used; our page for employers has a template you can use.

Fingerprint, face and Windows Hello checks happen inside the phone or PC. The apps only get a yes or no; they never receive or store biometric data, and neither do we.

On Android, scanning a join or team code uses Google's code scanner (part of Google Play services). The app never sees the camera picture, only the scanned text; Google may collect statistics about the scanner's use under Google's privacy policy.

What ZonoSoft's server receives

This applies to managers' devices running Zono Workforce and to team members' own phones and PCs running Zono Team.

  • When an app starts, signs in or signs out: a one-way scrambled code made from the device's own ID (not the ID itself), the app name and version, the type of device (such as "Windows PC" or "Android device", never the device's own name), the business name entered in the app, a code linking the devices of the same business, and the device's internet (IP) address. We use this to run the 30-day free trial fairly and to check licences.
  • Update checks (about every 6 hours while an app is open): the scrambled device code, app name, version, type of device and internet address. When an update is downloaded, we record which file was downloaded and by which (scrambled) device.
  • Licence use: which types of device use a licence, their app version and when each last checked in.
  • Your ZonoSoft account: business name, email address, your name if you choose to give it, the username and password you choose (the password is stored only as a secure one-way hash), your licences (plan, team members, paid-until date) and a record of when you agreed to our terms.
  • Website downloads: which account downloaded which installer, when, and from which internet address.
  • Payments: paid through Stripe (see below). We keep Stripe's reference numbers for your customer record and subscription, and the dates your licence is paid for. We never see your card or PayPal details.
  • Cookies: one cookie that keeps you signed in to your account for up to 12 hours. It's needed for the account to work, so we don't ask for consent. We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and this website loads nothing from other companies.

We only ask for what's needed to run your account: we don't ask for phone numbers, postal addresses or dates of birth. We don't send marketing emails, sell personal information or make automated decisions about you.

Why we use it (lawful basis)

  • To provide your account, licences, downloads and updates: performance of a contract with you.
  • To run the free trial fairly, prevent misuse of licences and keep the service secure: our legitimate interests.
  • To keep accounting and payment records: legal obligation.

How it's protected

The server is in the United Kingdom (IONOS). Names, email addresses and licence keys are encrypted in the database (AES-256), passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, connections use HTTPS, and access is limited to ZonoSoft. Backups are kept on the same server for 30 days.

How long we keep it

These are deleted automatically:

  • Server activity records (sign-ins, downloads, update checks): after 12 months.
  • Trial records for a device (the scrambled code and dates): 2 years after the device was last seen.
  • Records of a device that was removed from a licence: 2 years after it was last seen.
  • Backups: after 30 days.

Your account, licence, payment and terms-acceptance records are kept while you're a customer and for up to 6 years afterwards, for accounting and legal records. You can ask us to close your account sooner; we'll keep only what the law requires.

Who we share it with

  • IONOS runs our server in the UK on our behalf, under a data processing agreement.
  • Stripe takes card and PayPal payments. You give your payment details to Stripe on its own page, and Stripe tells us the payment was made. Stripe is responsible for the payment details it collects and may process them in the United States under UK-approved safeguards; see Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Anyone the law requires us to share it with.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for it in a portable format. You can change your contact details yourself in your account. To use any of these rights, email ZonoSoft@outlook.com or write to us at the address above. We'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first, by email, post or any other way. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it, keep you updated and tell you the outcome. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

We'll update this page when what we do changes, and show the date at the top. Important changes are also shown in your account.
