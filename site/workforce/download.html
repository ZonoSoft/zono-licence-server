Windows

  1. Download the installer and open it.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the steps. You don't need to be an administrator, and nothing else needs installing.
  4. Open the app from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.

To update, run the newer installer; your data is kept. To remove, use Settings, then Apps.

Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11.

Android

  1. Download the .apk on the phone or tablet.
  2. Open it. When asked, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Tap Install.

Android 6.0 or later. Zono Team installs alongside Zono Workforce. Updates install over the top and keep your data.

Zono isn't in the Microsoft Store or Google Play yet, so your device may warn you about an app from outside the store. Each file's SHA-256 fingerprint is listed above so you can check your download. Android apps are signed by ZonoSoft (certificate SHA-256 A7:96:D7:8B:…:62:A1:37).
