Guides

Step-by-step help to install and use the apps. Managers use Zono Workforce; team members use Zono Team.

Zono Workforce for WindowsFor managers on a Windows PC or laptop: install, set up your business, plan rotas, clocking, timesheets and payroll. Zono Workforce for AndroidFor managers on an Android phone or tablet: install, set up your business, plan rotas, clocking, timesheets and payroll. Zono Team for WindowsFor team members on a Windows PC or laptop: connect to your work, clock in and out and see your shifts. Zono Team for AndroidFor team members on an Android phone: connect to your work, clock in and out and see your shifts.

Employers: see using Zono Workforce with your staff for what to tell your team.
