About ZonoSoft

ZonoSoft is an independent software company in Dorset, in the south of England.

It started on a shop floor. Running a store meant hours every week on rotas, chasing clock-in times and adding up payroll hours by hand, with tools that were either spreadsheets or expensive systems built for head offices. Zono Workforce was written to do that job properly for small teams: one A4 rota, hours that follow on from it, and nothing to type twice.

Everything we make follows the same few rules:

  • Your business's records stay on your own devices and your own storage, encrypted. We never see them.
  • One plain price per business, with a free demo key so you can be sure first.
  • Clear, readable screens that work for everyone, including people with dyslexia or low vision.
  • Support from the people who build the software.
